## Core Factions

### Felid Corsairs (Cats — Player Faction)

- **ID:** `felid_corsairs`
- **Realm:** Realm of the Feline Courts
- **Species:** Cat
- **Ideology:** Freedom through power
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Independent
- **Government:** Decentralised Captains
- **Politics:** Meritocracy — leadership earned, not inherited
- **Culture:** Crew loyalty earned through loot and leadership, not rank. Fiercely self-determined.
- **Ships:** Fast, low armour, high manoeuvrability. Asymmetric, jury-rigged, devastatingly effective.
- **Special:** Sole producers of Whisper Crystals. This is their power and their target.
- **Abilities:** Stealth, speed, cunning diplomacy
  - **Crystal Refining** — only faction that can refine raw crystals
  - **Shadow Running** — evade detection in hostile territory (-30% ambush rate)
  - **Cunning Diplomacy** — unlocks bluff/misdirect dialogue options
- **Base Ship Stats:** Speed 8 | Armour 3 | Firepower 5 | Crystal Capacity 6 | Crew 4
- **Key NPCs:** Aristotle (leader), Death (rival)
- **Origin:** The Long Run — 300 years ago. Strays fled Lion oppression in a mass exodus led by Tessera the Uncounted, a three-legged matriarch who refused every name a Lion gave her. They stole decommissioned merchant ships and fled to the Fringe.
- **Founding Figure:** Tessera the Uncounted. The crystal network remembers her. Aristotle is her ninth-generation descendant (neither he nor his mother know this).
- **Internal Dissenter:** Aristotle himself — by becoming something larger than the Corsairs, he is the crack in their own monolith.
- **Living Memory:** The Lions remember the Long Run as a rebellion. The Corsairs remember it as an escape. Both are right. This is the historical wound at the heart of the cat civil war that has never quite ended.

### Canis League (Dogs)

- **ID:** `canis_league`
- **Realm:** Realm of the Canine Order
- **Species:** Dog
- **Ideology:** Order through loyalty
- **Alignment:** Lawful Hierarchical
- **Government:** Military Command
- **Politics:** Military hierarchy and pack loyalty
- **Culture:** Loyalty, honour, hierarchy. They follow orders. Naval power structure.
- **Ships:** Bulkier, stronger armour, more guns. Organised fleet formations.
- **Special:** Completely crystal-dependent. This drives their aggression.
- **Abilities:** Organised warfare, siege tactics, superior logistics
  - **Organised Warfare** — fleet formations grant +15% armour when 2+ ships present
  - **Siege Tactics** — can blockade supply routes (-50% crystal throughput)
  - **Superior Logistics** — resupply/repair at League ports costs 20% less
- **Base Ship Stats:** Speed 4 | Armour 8 | Firepower 7 | Crystal Capacity 5 | Crew 8
- **Key NPCs:** Dave (commander)
- **Origin:** Founded during the Crystal Famine (200 years ago) by General Howl — a working-class mastiff who built a cooperative militia to prevent starvation. Her original charter is preserved in a sealed archive. Dave has read it. After her death, the aristocratic Venator family captured the League from within, centralised command, and rewrote the founding documents to emphasise obedience over loyalty.
- **Founding Figure:** General Howl. Her descendants Jessamine and Toma Howl carry her vision forward. Admiral Vara Venator is the great-great-granddaughter of the man who ruined it.
- **Internal Dissenter:** Brigadier Jessamine Howl — organising reform from within, offering Dave a third path.
- **Living Memory:** Dave knows what the League was supposed to be. That knowledge is what makes his loyalty crisis possible.

### The Lions (Noble Cat Hierarchy)

- **ID:** `lions`
- **Realm:** Realm of the Feline Courts (noble branch)
- **Species:** Cat (Noble)
- **Ideology:** Rule by divine birthright
- **Alignment:** Lawful Aristocratic
- **Government:** Noble Hierarchy
- **Politics:** They were rulers before crystals existed and intend to rule after. Aristotle is an insult to their lineage.
- **Culture:** Self-declared noblest beasts of the jungle. Arrogant, political, claim divine right.
- **Ships:** Ornate, balanced stats with political modifications.
- **Special:** Claim birthright over Whisper Crystals. Pressure Aristotle from within cat hierarchy.
- **Abilities:** Diplomatic pressure, noble authority, royal decree
  - **Diplomatic Pressure** — force tribute demands costing crystals if refused
  - **Noble Authority** — +20% political influence over neutral factions in their regions
  - **Royal Decree** — can temporarily close trade routes to hostile factions
- **Base Ship Stats:** Speed 5 | Armour 6 | Firepower 5 | Crystal Capacity 7 | Crew 6
- **Tension:** Need Aristotle's knowledge but resent owing anything to a street cat.
- **Origin:** Pre-spaceflight civilisation, 100,000+ years. Built around the Prides — dynasties from the mythological Twelve Manes. Current civil war caused by the poisoning of Crown Prince Thorold (the progressive heir). His brother Rhodion (Lord Mane) is fighting the war to finish what Thorold started.
- **Internal Dissenter:** Lord Mane (Rhodion of House Amber) — a younger brother who was supposed to read poetry and marry well, slowly becoming a leader worthy of his brother.
- **Key Secret:** Blood Paw knows who poisoned Thorold. Lady Penumbra (real name Obsidia Vale) is using the civil war to settle a forty-year family vendetta.

### The Wolves (Military Elite Dogs)

- **ID:** `wolves`
- **Realm:** Realm of the Canine Order (military elite branch)
- **Species:** Wolf
- **Ideology:** Dominance through superior tactics and evolutionary right
- **Alignment:** Lawful Dominant
- **Government:** Military Stratocracy
- **Politics:** Self-declared noblest beasts of the forest. They do not follow orders — they give them.
- **Culture:** Strategic, disciplined, utterly convinced of superiority. Dominance and calculated power.
- **Ships:** Tactical, optimised for combat. The toughest opponents in space.
- **Special:** Want to break the crystal monopoly by force. The strategic mind behind Canis League military ambition.
- **Abilities:** Tactical superiority, pack coordination, evolutionary right
  - **Tactical Superiority** — +20% firepower when initiating combat (first-strike)
  - **Pack Coordination** — multiple Wolf ships share targeting data (-15% player dodge)
  - **Evolutionary Right** — diplomatic encounters can escalate to combat with no reputation penalty
- **Base Ship Stats:** Speed 6 | Armour 7 | Firepower 8 | Crystal Capacity 4 | Crew 7
- **Origin:** The defence of Drennhal (100 years ago). The first Iron Fang led planetary defence against an alien incursion. The name became a title; the current Iron Fang (6th) is a large, tired male who wears it like an inherited debt. The Hunt Eternal culture it produced has the highest suicide rate in the multiverse.
- **Internal Dissenter:** Kestrel — Iron Fang's daughter, 16, fleeing Wolf culture. Luna (Iron Fang's niece) is also a dissenter, now navigating for Dave.
- **Living Memory:** Wolf poetry is almost uniformly about exhaustion. The culture knows it is unsustainable. No one knows how to stop.

## Wider Multiverse Factions

### Fairies

- **ID:** `fairies`
- **Realm:** Fairy Realms
- **Species:** Fairy
- **Ideology:** Knowledge is currency
- **Politics:** Fluid mercantile allegiances
- **Role:** Magical traders and information brokers
- **Personality:** Know more than they let on. Mystical, enigmatic, transactional.
- **Abilities:** Magical espionage, enchanted goods, intelligence networks
- **Gameplay:** Offer intel, rare trades, and unique crystal-related knowledge. High-value but unpredictable allies.
- **Origin:** The oldest witnesses. Their civilisation was taught by Solenne (the last Builder) how to hear crystals without burning them. Built around the Heart Garden — a pocket dimension archive where crystalline flowers record everything spoken near them. Some still hum with Solenne's voice.
- **Reproduction:** Fairies bud — every living fairy carries scraps of every fairy who came before. Death distributes their memories rather than ending them.
- **Founding Figure / Leader:** Ember-Who-Remembers — one being in twelve bodies. Named in Arc 5.
- **Internal Dissenter:** Moth — the youngest fairy bud, frightened of the wider multiverse, keeper of the oldest crystal fragment (Solenne's last words).
- **Key NPCs:** Ember-Who-Remembers, Moth, Silky (half-fairy crew member), Pip the Cartographer

### Knights

- **ID:** `knights`
- **Realm:** Knight Kingdoms
- **Species:** Human (chivalric orders)
- **Ideology:** Order through law
- **Politics:** Feudal hierarchy imposing universal order
- **Role:** Order-seekers from medieval realms
- **Personality:** Honourable, rigid, believe in imposing structure on chaos.
- **Abilities:** Heavy armour, disciplined formations, siege engineering
- **Gameplay:** Military alliance at cost of autonomy. Strong combat support but political strings attached.
- **Origin:** Cordance Massacre (400 years ago). A Lion punitive expedition over a tribute dispute devastated the mixed-species realm of Cordance. Survivors built the Last Dawn Oath: order, honour, mutual obligation above all. Their rigidity is a trauma response.
- **Founding Figure:** The survivors of Cordance collectively. Current Grand Marshal: Ser Galvain (badger), who lost his own family 30 years ago and has been married to the Oath since.
- **Internal Dissenter:** Brother Ashen — a young Knight asking whether the Oath can survive contact with a multiverse that has moved on.
- **Key NPCs:** Ser Galvain (Grand Marshal), Thistle/Briar Galvain (his estranged niece, Dave's crew), Brother Ashen

### Goblins

- **ID:** `goblins`
- **Realm:** Goblin Warrens
- **Species:** Goblin
- **Ideology:** Profit above all
- **Politics:** Anarchic guild cartels
- **Role:** Scavengers, engineers, black-market dealers
- **Personality:** Resourceful, amoral, profit-driven. Will deal with anyone. Will cheat you if they can, then buy you a drink if the cheat was clever.
- **Abilities:** Rapid ship modification, black-market crystal trade, sabotage services
- **Gameplay:** Ship upgrades, black-market crystal trades, illicit goods. Useful but unreliable.
- **Origin:** No founding myth — goblins don't believe in myths. Emerged from a federation of scrap-scavenging crews who figured out that fixing things was more profitable than stealing them. Governed by rotating "Deal Chiefs" elected by profit margin.
- **Key Figure:** Madam Sprocket — current Deal Chief. Made her fortune selling League warships back to the League under different paint jobs. Most goblins Aristotle meets are Sprocket relatives. The clan has been manoeuvring to get close to him since they heard about refined crystals.
- **Internal Dissenter:** Yarrow — the only goblin who refuses to sell out. A pariah. Secretly the most reliable goblin in the story.

### Aliens & Space Races

- **ID:** `aliens`
- **Realm:** Deep Space
- **Species:** Various
- **Ideology:** Varies wildly by race
- **Politics:** Varies wildly by race
- **Role:** Advanced civilisations with their own agendas
- **Personality:** Wild cards — could be allies, enemies, or something entirely unexpected. But not hostile — desperate. They have been trying to save the crystal network for millions of years and failing for most of that time.
- **Abilities:** Superior technology, unknown weapons, deeply unpredictable behaviour
- **Gameplay:** Introduce multiverse scale. Unique technology offers. Unpredictable faction dynamics.
- **Origin:** An ancient preservation coalition — not a single faction but a loose group of species who share one priority: preventing the crystal network from dying.
- **Key Figure:** The Void Singer — not a species but a position. The current one has been the Void Singer for 60,000 years, looks like drifting light, was once physical.
- **Internal Dissenter:** Kiln — 5,000 years old, embedded in the multiverse for 600 years, has grown attached to the multiverse she was sent to save. Speaks normally. Willing to be honest. The alien you can have dinner with.
- **Narrative role:** The aliens are running out of potential stewards. Aristotle is not their first choice — he is their latest choice.

## Deep History — The Builders and Solenne

*This lore surfaces gradually through logs, Heart Garden archives, Ember's dialogue, Crystal Communion, and ultimately the Cradle in Arc 9. It should never be dumped on the player in monologue.*

### The Builders (≈4.2 billion years ago)
Twelve beings who had already outgrown their home galaxy. They found resonant silicate in the mantle of a dying star and cultivated it — teaching it to hold memory, to echo, to sing. They planted it across thirteen regions. Over millions of years, the seeds grew into a crystal network that connected every realm.

Eleven Builders merged their consciousness with the crystal lattice. The twelfth stayed behind to tend the garden. Her name was **Solenne**.

### Solenne (≈4 billion years ago to ≈1 billion years ago)
For two billion years, Solenne walked the realms in a body that renewed itself, teaching early civilisations how to hear the crystals without exploiting them. The Fairies were her oldest students. Pip the Cartographer carries a fragment of her records without knowing what it is.

When a species discovered that raw crystal could be burned for propulsion, Solenne tried to stop the first burn fleets. She is believed to have died in this period. Her consciousness partially merged with the network — scattered fragments, most of them silent. Her final words echo in the crystal lattice: *"Listen. Please. Just listen."*

### The Crystal Network's State (Present Day)
The network has been running on fumes for 500 million years. The Fairy elders have known this for a thousand years. Without intervention, the jump gates will begin to fail within decades. The network will accept a steward — someone it recognises as a listener — who can help it heal.

Aristotle is the first potential steward in a thousand generations to apologise to a crystal before using it. The network remembers.

## Conquest & Competition

Every faction is actively competing to become the dominant power across all realms. The goal for every faction is conquest and total control of the multiverse. Aristotle sits at the centre because whoever controls the crystals controls who can participate in that race.

**Mixed-Universe Crews:** Ship crews are NOT faction-pure. Any captain's crew can contain members from across the multiverse. A cat captain can have goblin engineers, fairy scouts, and a knight navigator. Crew composition affects ship capabilities, encounter options, and morale dynamics.
